Neo-Veridia Midnight Circuit

Neo-Veridia · 2099

NEO-VERIDIA

MIDNIGHT CIRCUIT

Illegal hover races under the neon. Drift to charge three boost tiers, slipstream rivals, grab Data Cores and dodge the hazards. Eight cars, one 4.5 km circuit.

Items

Drive through the glowing cyan Data Cores on the road to pick up an item. It shows at the top centre. Press E (or tap ITEM) to use it.

Pulse MissileHoming shot at the nearest car ahead
EMP BurstSlows every car close to you
Aegis ShieldBlocks the next hit for 6 s
Phase ShiftPass through hazards and cars for 2.5 s
OverclockBig speed boost for 3 s
Heat SinkRefills 50 energy

Hazards

A warning appears with the side to steer to. Oil costs grip, EMP barriers pulse on and off, and scaffolding drops when you pass fast. A short grace period follows every hit. Rivals get hit too. Ram a rival from behind at speed for a takedown bonus. Steering assist keeps you off the walls.

Tips

Hold a drift to charge: blue at 0.8 s, orange at 1.8 s, magenta at 3.0 s. Release for the boost. Sit behind a car to draft. Drive through the green ring with 70+ energy or a Super-Turbo charge to take the shortcut.
